# Production Operations

The canonical, versioned operations and incident-response policy is maintained in the repository:

**[Production operations runbooks](https://github.com/valence-works/groundwork-v2/blob/main/docs/v2/production-operations.md)**

That document owns deployment checklists, incident evidence, provider recovery procedures, and the
boundary between deployment-owner and Groundwork-maintainer responsibility. Keeping those safety
instructions in one place prevents the wiki from drifting into a different production promise.

The matching topology tiers are defined by the
**[provider support matrix](https://github.com/valence-works/groundwork-v2/blob/main/docs/v2/support-matrix.md)**.
